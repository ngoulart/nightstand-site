The phone rule. The page applies the app's rule to each lane's own bins, night by night (6 pm to noon on the phone's clock): a rest is 30 minutes or more of still bins, a minute of movement inside it is ignored, two rests no more than 5 minutes apart with every bin between them recorded are one night, and the night with the most rest counts from two hours up. Efficiency is the rest over the rest plus the interruptions. It needs the 1-minute interval, follows the still threshold above, and reads only the part of a night inside the window. Reading a lane. A recording is armed for 12 hours at every open, so a phone that opened the app at bedtime has a lane that runs to the morning once it opens again. Gray at the right end is normal until the next open or background refresh; gray for a whole night means the recording was not armed or the upload has not come in yet.
