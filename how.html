From a still phone to a night of sleep

While you sleep, your phone lies still. NightStand reads that stillness as your night.

The app records every night on its own. It reads how your phone moves and keeps a 30-second summary.

At night, it estimates your sleep from how long the phone lies still, and takes off the short interruptions when you pick it up.

If you wear an Apple Watch, it also reads the sleep in Health.

What a night tells you

How long you slept. The hours the phone lay at rest, less the interruptions.

How regular your nights are. When you fell asleep and woke up, and over weeks how much that varies.

How well you slept. Each interruption with its time, and the efficiency: the share of the night asleep.

What came before. How long the phone moved in the hour before the night started.

You guess before you see

Each morning the app asks about your night first. Then it shows your guess against the measured night.

The morning check-in asks how you slept, when you fell asleep and for how long, with four short questions about the night and one 30-second reaction test.

Two reminders a day, one in the morning and one in the evening. Each has its own switch and time in Settings.

How sleep shows the next day

Six 30-second tests of alertness, memory and focus, in the morning, before bed or any time. Trends set each score against your usual and against the night before.

Reaction

Alertness

A counter starts at random moments. Tap as fast as you can when it starts.

Symbols

Processing speed

A symbol appears in the middle. Tap its number from the key at the top.

2-back

Working memory

Letters appear one at a time. Tap Match when a letter is the same as the one two letters before.

Go / no-go

Impulse control

Tap as soon as the green circle appears, and hold back on the pink square.

Sequence

Memory span

Pads light up in a sequence. Tap them in the same order. Each round adds one.

Colors

Focus

A color word appears. Tap the color of its ink, as fast as you can.

Your sleep over weeks

Trends show your average night, how much the time you fall asleep varies, your efficiency, your movement and your test scores, by day, week, month and six months.

What it asks your iPhone for

Each has a switch in the app's Settings.

Motion & Fitness. How much the phone moved, in 30-second summaries. The night is measured from this.

Health. The sleep an Apple Watch wrote, if you wear one.

Location. Keeps the app recording through the night, so the night has no gaps.

Notifications. The morning and evening reminders.
