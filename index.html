NightStand is a sleep health app for iPhone. It measures your night from the phone on your nightstand and shows what your sleep does to your day.

Why sleep

What research says about the hours and the regularity of sleep. Each card opens its source.

7h+ 7 hours or more

Sleep medicine societies recommend that adults sleep 7 or more hours a night on a regular basis.

Watson et al. 2015, Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine 2 weeks Sleep debt adds up

Two weeks of 6 hours or less in bed a night hurt thinking as much as up to two sleepless nights, and people seemed largely unaware of it.

Van Dongen et al. 2003, Sleep 61,000 Regular beats long

Among about 61,000 UK adults, a regular sleep schedule predicted the risk of death better than how long people slept.

Windred et al. 2024, Sleep 0.05% Tired like tipsy

After 17 to 19 hours awake, people did as badly or worse on some tests as at a 0.05% blood alcohol level.

Williamson and Feyer 2000, Occupational and Environmental Medicine

Each morning, it asks about your night before it shows the measurement: how you slept, when you fell asleep and for how long.
