Two check-ins a day

Evening

One 30-second test before bed.

Morning

About 90 seconds, with a 30-second test. Then you see how you slept. While sharing is on, the test is part of every check-in.

What leaves your phone

Sent while sharing is on

  • How the phone moves, in 30-second summaries
  • The sleep in Health, if an Apple Watch wrote it
  • Your check-ins and test results
  • When the phone charges, locks and unlocks
  • Your phone's model, iOS version, app version, time zone and language setting

Your location, the apps you use and what is on your screen are never sent. The privacy policy has the full list.

Under your study code only. The data go to our study's database over an encrypted connection. Only the study team can read them.

Stopping. Taking part is voluntary. Turn the switch off and the app stops sending at once. Data sent before then may still be analysed.

Deletion. Write to nicolasgoulartdemoura@gmail.com with your study code and we delete what you shared.
